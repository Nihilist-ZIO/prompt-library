Greetings! Today, I'd like to present a short guide on what angles and perspectives I use for generating images.
Bird's eye view: A shot taken from above, showing a scene from above.
Worm's eye view: A photo taken from below, looking up from the ground, works the same as a low angle.
Close up: A shot showing details of a subject, such as a face, in close focus.
Extreme close up: A very close shot that captures a specific detail, such as an eye, in focus.
Upper body / Cowboy Shot: A shot from the waist up of the subject.
Dutch angle: An angle shot that creates a dynamic, distorted perspective.
High angle: A shot that looks at the subject from above.
Low angle: A shot that looks at the subject from below.
Frontal view: The subject is looking at the camera
Back view / Rear view: The subject has their back to the camera
Side view: The subject is shot from the side
You can also add the following to the prompt to get a random angle:
Random angle, dynamic angle
Have fun creating!
