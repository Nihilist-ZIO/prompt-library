Hey all! So I know a lot of you struggle when prompting for backgrounds, and I'd like to give you a few tips to help you achieve what you're looking for!
Template Formula
We're going to use this template to make it easier when prompting for backgrounds, of course, you don't have to fill in all of these, it's just an idea to get you going!
[indoor/outdoor], [theme/style], [key background location], [important objects or structures], [time of day], [weather], [lighting/mood adjectives], [small background details]

🧠 Bonus Tips for Background Prompting
Mention time of day : morning, dusk, night, midnight, dawn.
Weather adds mood: rainy, snowy, foggy, stormy, sunny, dusty.
Use adjectives to strengthen the scene: "ruined", "elegant", "oppressive", "overgrown", "desolate", "bustling".
Think of lighting : neon lights, candlelight, moonlight, glowing fog, flickering torches, cyber glow.
Use 2-3 small details to make scenes vivid without overloading.
