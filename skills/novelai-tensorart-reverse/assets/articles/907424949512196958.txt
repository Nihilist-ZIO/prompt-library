Model + Quality Prompts
Using Diviling-Illustrious Anime: https://tensor.art/models/897826979653397911
Positive prompt : (masterpiece, best quality, absurdres, highres), amazing quality, very aesthetic, (volumetric lighting), digital art style, drawn aesthetic, concept painting, ultrahigh definition,
Negative prompt : worst quality, early, low quality, lowres, signature, username, logo, bad hands, mutated hands, mammal, anthro, furry, ambiguous form, feral, semi-anthro, boring, ugly
