Dear Creators,
Thank you for being a vital part of the TensorArt community.
To foster innovation and long-term ecosystem health, we’re refining our resource focus. Starting July 22, 2026 (UTC) , creator incentives will be restructured into three parallel earning tracks :
🔄 Key Updates: Three Earning Tracks
Moving forward, your daily incentive earnings will be calculated as: TenStar Fund + Pro Model Incentives + Pro Tools Incentives .
1️⃣ TenStar Fund (EXCLUSIVE / EARLY ACCESS)
Threshold Adjustments: Standard TenStar Fund incentives will now only apply to New Base Models , and revenue is counted only from Pro user usage .
The current eligible New Base Models are limited to Anima and Krea2 (subject to dynamic updates; we will announce any additions promptly via Discord—please stay tuned!).
The rules and interactions for "EXCLUSIVE / EARLY ACCESS" on other non-currently-defined New Base Models remain unchanged, but they will no longer generate TenStar Fund revenue.
2️⃣ Pro Model Incentives
Regardless of whether it is a New Base Model, approved Pro Models will retain member incentives and referral bonuses ($3 per referral).
Only revenue from Pro user usage is counted, with the coefficient consistent with TenStar Fund .
3️⃣ Pro Tools Incentives
Approved Pro Tools will retain member incentives and referral bonuses ($3 per referral).
Only revenue from Pro user usage is counted, with the coefficient consistent with TenStar Fund .
Standard Tools without Pro segment enabled will no longer receive incentives.
⚠️ Impact on You
This adjustment applies only to the main TensorArt site .
🌱 Hub 🌱 remains unchanged — All existing Hub rules and features continue as before.
✅ What Stays the Same
Buffet Plan Settings: You can still enable paid access for your models or tools via the Buffet Plan.
Open Applications: Submissions for EXCLUSIVE / EARLY ACCESS / Pro Models and Pro Tools remain open with full benefits intact.
🔮 What’s Next
This shift accelerates our next-gen model ecosystem. Soon, we’ll roll out enhanced Forge Agents , new templates, and a dedicated Forge Creator Incentive Program —ensuring your creativity earns more direct, sustainable rewards.
