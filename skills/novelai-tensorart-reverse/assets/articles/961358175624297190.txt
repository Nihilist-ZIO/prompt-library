✨ Creating Love and Deepspace Characters with Illustrious XL
Prompt Guide for Zayne, Xavier, Sylus & Rafayel 💫
Illustrious XL is an excellent model for creating refined, emotional anime-style portraits.
Its strength lies in soft lighting, elegant facial features, and painterly details — making it a perfect match for Love and Deepspace characters.
Below is a clean and balanced prompt structure for generating the main male characters, optimized for half-body or portrait compositions . 💖
🌿 Zayne — Calm, Elegant, and Mysterious
Zayne’s appearance is defined by restrained elegance and subtle intensity. Dark tones and soft shadows help emphasize his composed personality.
Prompt:
zayne (love and deepspace), love and deepspace,
1boy, lips,
green eyes, brown eyes,
short hair, black hair, parted bangs,
collared shirt, black shirt, white shirt,
long sleeves
✨ Tip: Use soft, low-contrast lighting to enhance his calm and mature aura.
❄️ Xavier — Gentle and Ethereal
Xavier works beautifully with light colors and a clean, airy atmosphere. Illustrious XL highlights his softness and dreamlike presence.
Prompt:
xavier (love and deepspace), love and deepspace,
1boy, lips,
blue eyes,
short hair, grey hair,
white shirt, collared shirt,
long sleeves
✨ Tip: Cool lighting and minimal backgrounds enhance his serene expression.
🩸 Sylus — Dark Charm and Intensity
Sylus thrives in dramatic lighting. His red eyes and darker clothing create a striking contrast that Illustrious XL renders especially well.
Prompt:
sylus (love and deepspace), love and deepspace,
1boy, lips,
red eyes,
short hair, grey hair, parted bangs,
collared shirt, black shirt
✨ Tip: Add subtle shadows around the eyes for a more dangerous, seductive look.
🌸 Rafayel — Dreamy and Artistic
Rafayel’s purple palette pairs perfectly with Illustrious XL’s soft pastel rendering. He looks best in luminous, gentle compositions.
Prompt:
rafayel (love and deepspace), love and deepspace,
1boy, lips,
purple eyes,
purple hair, short hair, parted bangs,
white shirt, collared shirt,
long sleeves
✨ Tip: Floral elements or glowing accents enhance his romantic aesthetic.
