When you're prompting for image generation, you can guide the model's attention to focus on specific parts of the image. This is called focal emphasis.
Prompting focus tells the AI which part of the image should be emphasized or featured most prominently in the composition. It affects framing, detail level, and where the viewer's eye is drawn.
