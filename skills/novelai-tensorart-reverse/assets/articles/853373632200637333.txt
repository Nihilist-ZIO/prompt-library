Beginning
Hand Gesture have always been a very challenging aspect to control. I wish to share some highly effective prompts for controlling Gestures.
The tested Model is ✨🎨Illustrious - AnimeMaster✨ and these prompts perform well on most models.
At the end of the article, there are Demonstration Images showing the effects.
If you want to watch Video Tutorial (which is more efficient and intuitive), you can visit 👉 https://www.instagram.com/p/DIeFERbygkO/?hl=zh-cn
